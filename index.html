Privacy Policy

Aurora Live Wallpapers are 3D and interactive live wallpaper applications for Android.

Aurora Live Wallpapers do not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell personal information.

The applications do not require user accounts and do not use advertising, analytics, location tracking, or third-party services for collecting personal information.

Local Preferences

Preferences selected within the applications, such as wallpaper appearance, colors, fog, glow, animation, character, and other customization settings, are stored locally on the user's device and are used only to configure the live wallpapers.

Children's Privacy

Aurora Live Wallpapers do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If the data practices of Aurora Live Wallpapers change in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.
