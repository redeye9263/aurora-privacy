The Haunted Lantern

Privacy Policy

The Haunted Lantern is a 3D live wallpaper for Android.

The Haunted Lantern does not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell personal information.

The application does not require user accounts and does not use advertising, analytics, location tracking, or third-party services for collecting personal information.

Local Preferences

Preferences selected within the application, such as wallpaper appearance, colors, fog, glow, and animation settings, are stored locally on the user's device and are used only to configure the live wallpaper.

Children's Privacy

The Haunted Lantern does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If the application's data practices change in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.
